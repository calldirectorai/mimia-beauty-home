Questions

Before you book.

Is a wellness service right for me?

That is a question for the provider. Please do not share medical or health details in the website form or chat. Book a wellness consultation, or call the spa and the team will connect you with the right person.

Can I book this like a facial or wax?

Wellness appointments are booked separately, from the wellness booking link on this page.

Is the website chat able to advise me?

No. The chat can share hours, location and booking links, and can ask the team to call you back. It does not give medical advice.
