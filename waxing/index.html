Questions

How long does a Brazilian wax last?

A Brazilian wax typically lasts 3 to 4 weeks, depending on your hair growth cycle. Regular waxing helps hair grow back finer and slower.

How often should I get a Brazilian wax?

The spa recommends every 4 to 6 weeks. Consistent waxing helps maintain smooth skin and keeps the hair growth cycle more manageable.

Does a Brazilian wax hurt?

Some discomfort is normal, especially the first time. Appointments become easier with regular visits because hair grows back finer. The spa uses a gentle technique and high-quality wax.

Which Brazilian wax should I book?

First-time guests book the First-Time Brazilian Wax. If you were waxed here within the last 4 to 6 weeks, book the 4 to 6 week appointment. If it has been longer than 6 weeks, book the over-6-weeks appointment so there is enough time.
